Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Keepy Uppy!, developed and published under the name Aveniq Studios.

Aveniq Studios is the developer name used by an individual developer. This policy explains how information may be processed when you use Keepy Uppy!.

1. Information We Collect

Keepy Uppy! does not require users to create an account or register. We do not operate a user account system or an individual player profile database.

We do not ask users to enter personal information directly into the game.

2. Local Game Data

Game progress and preferences, such as high scores and unlocked cosmetic items, may be stored locally on your device.

This locally stored information is not automatically uploaded to an Aveniq Studios player account or player database. It may be lost if you uninstall the game or delete its app data.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

Keepy Uppy! uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements.

In connection with advertising services, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Device and app information
  • Approximate location information derived from technical data
  • Advertising interactions
  • Technical diagnostic and fraud-prevention information

The information processed depends on your device, consent choices, applicable settings and the advertising services being used.

Depending on the specific processing activity, Google and Aveniq Studios may have different responsibilities under applicable data protection laws. Google may act as an independent controller for certain activities and as a processor for others.

Further information is available in Google's Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy .

4. Consent Management

Keepy Uppy! uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising-related consent choices where required.

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be presented with options to consent, reject consent or manage your privacy preferences.

The game is configured not to request advertisements when the required consent has not been obtained or when the consent status cannot be established.

The core gameplay remains available without watching advertisements.

Where available and required, privacy options can also be accessed from within the game to review or change your choices.

5. Data Sharing

Aveniq Studios does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners in connection with the advertising services integrated into the game. Their processing is subject to their applicable privacy policies and settings.

Aveniq Studios does not operate a separate player analytics service or user account service as part of the game.

6. Data Retention

Locally stored game data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app data.

Advertising-related information is retained according to the applicable practices and retention policies of Google and its advertising partners.

7. Children's Privacy

Keepy Uppy! is a general-audience game and is not specifically designed or marketed to children.

The game does not require users to provide personal information directly. Advertising services may nevertheless process technical information as described in this policy.

Advertising and audience settings are configured separately through the relevant platform services and must comply with applicable requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing activities.

You can contact us regarding personal information for which Aveniq Studios may be responsible. We will assess your request and respond in accordance with applicable data protection law.

For information processed directly by Google, you can also consult Google's privacy information and controls: Google Privacy Policy .

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the game and its integrated services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, its services or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page together with the date of the latest update.
